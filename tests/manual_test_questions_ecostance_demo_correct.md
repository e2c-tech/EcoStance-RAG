## 📊 Testing Instructions

### Systematic Testing Approach:
1. **Start with Company Basics** (Category 1) - Verify core information retrieval
2. **Test Policy Documents** (Categories 2-5) - Check document-specific queries
3. **Analyze Sales Data** (Category 6) - Test numerical and analytical capabilities
4. **Cross-Reference** (Categories 8-10) - Test multi-document reasoning

### Quality Assessment Criteria:
- ✅ **Accuracy**: Information matches source documents
- ✅ **Completeness**: Comprehensive answers with relevant details
- ✅ **Clarity**: Well-structured, easy to understand responses
- ✅ **Source Attribution**: Clear indication of information source
- ⚠️ **Partial**: Some relevant information but incomplete
- ❌ **Incorrect**: Wrong information or misinterpretation

### Performance Metrics to Track:
- **Response Time**: How quickly answers are generated
- **Answer Quality**: Accuracy and completeness of responses
- **Source Relevance**: How well retrieved context matches the query
- **Consistency**: Similar questions should yield consistent answers
